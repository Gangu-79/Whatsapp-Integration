Why is Meta API Access Blocked? (Error 200 Resolution)

Meta Cloud API returns Error 200: "API access blocked" when the System User token has not been granted Full Control of your WhatsApp Business Account (WABA), or when your Meta payment method requires review.

Follow these exact 3 steps in Meta Business Suite:
1. Assign WABA Asset: Go to Business Settings → Users → System Users. Select your user, click "Add Assets", select "WhatsApp Accounts", check your account, and toggle "Manage WhatsApp account (Full control)" to ON.
2. Verify Payment Status: Go to Billing & Payments and ensure your card has completed authorization and has no unpaid hold.
3. Permissions Check: Ensure your System User token was generated with whatsapp_business_messaging and whatsapp_business_management.
